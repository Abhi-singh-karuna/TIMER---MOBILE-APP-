Live Focus redesign plan

Live Focus is your day as a horizontal timeline. It is the most distinctive screen in the app, and also the largest and busiest. This page lists what is there today, what makes it hard to use, three redesign directions you can compare, and a checklist of ideas. Tick the ones you want. Ticks are saved in this browser.

How Live Focus works today

  • TIMELINEA horizontal day with a live NOW line that moves every quarter second. Pinch to zoom (minutes per cell), scroll sideways, and your zoom and position are remembered.
  • BLOCKSEach subtask is a block you can drag to move and resize. Blocks sit in lanes, and tapping one opens a status popup (Upcoming, In Process, Done, Undone).
  • 3 VIEWSTask view (one row per task), Merged view (all subtasks in one row) and Category view (rows grouped by category). The task name column on the left can be hidden.
  • DOCKA bottom bar that expands. It holds the running timer, a date picker, the Now button, the view switcher and a full-screen timer.
  • POPUPSAdd subtask, approval, stage status, task subtasks, view mode and calendar are all separate popups. Portrait uses a different dock layout from landscape.
  • CODELiveFocusView.tsx is 6,509 lines, the largest file in the app. Props arrive from Task/index.tsx and App.tsx.

What makes it hard to use

"Seen" means I saw it in the code. "Check" means I expect it from the code, and you should confirm it on your phone.

  • SEENToo many controls. Three view modes, a hideable task column, an expanding dock, six popups and a full-screen timer all compete for a small landscape screen.
  • SEENAuto-scroll to NOW stops once you scroll. The code tracks "user has scrolled" and then stops following. After that you need the Now button to find the present again.
  • SEENView names are unclear. "Merged" and "Task" do not tell you what you will see. There is no preview of the three modes.
  • SEENMoving and resizing are separate modes. There is a "resize mode" state, so editing a block is hidden behind a press or a long press. A new user will not discover it.
  • CHECKThe day is shown, but "what now and next" is not. You see all hours, so the current stage and the next stage are small blocks among many.
  • CHECKPerformance. The NOW line re-renders every 250 ms inside one giant component. On older phones, this can make dragging feel heavy.
  • CHECKStatus colours and meaning. Blocks use status colours (grey, orange, green, red), but it is hard to tell a missed block from a not-yet-started one at a glance.
  • SEENHard to change. Everything lives in one file, so even a small design change is risky.

Decide before starting

  1. Direction A (playhead and side panel), B (agenda first) or C (cleaned-up timeline)? C is the safest, A is my pick.
  2. Main use Do you open Live Focus mostly to plan the day, or to stay on what you are doing now? Planning favours C, staying on task favours A or B.
  3. Views Keep all three views (task, merged, category) or reduce to two?
  4. Portrait Should Live Focus be redesigned for portrait too, or stay landscape-first?
  5. Ideas Which ideas above do you want? I can group them into phases once you choose.
