Goals improvement plan

Goals exist in the app, but they work like a report: you link tasks and watch a percentage. Nothing tells you what to do next, the target settings you enter are never used, and some numbers are wrong or made up. This plan fixes the numbers first, then makes goals useful, then redesigns the screens. Tick items as you finish them. Ticks are saved in this browser.

How goals work today

  • WHEREThird tab in the top switch (Timer, Task, Goal). Code: GoalManagement/index.tsx (4,369 lines) and AddGoalModal.tsx (2,392 lines). State and save handlers are in App.tsx; data is stored under @goals.
  • MODELA goal has a title, description, card color, dates, a type, an optional parent, linked task ids and target settings (hours per day, days per week, monthly goal).
  • CREATEOne large modal: title, description, start and end date, card color, pick tasks by category or date range, and target settings.
  • SHOWA card per top-level goal with a progress bar, a status pill (Upcoming, Running, Completed, Failed), linked tasks, an activity graph and monthly analytics.
  • PROGRESSCalculated on screen from linked tasks: the share of stages marked Done, or the share of completed tasks if there are no stages.

Why it feels unused

Ordered by how much each one hurts. "Where" shows the place in the code I checked.

  • HIGHTarget settings are collected but never used. Hours per day, days per week and the monthly goal are saved, then ignored, so a goal cannot say "you are behind this week".AddGoalModal saves targetSettings; GoalManagement never reads it.
  • HIGHNo "what do I do next". A goal does not show its next task, and tasks do not show which goal they belong to. You have to open the Goal tab to see anything.
  • HIGHRecurring tasks are counted wrongly. Progress and done counts read the base task, but a recurring task keeps status and stages per date. A daily habit can show 0% or 100% by mistake.renderDashboardCard uses task.stages and task.status directly.
  • HIGHDates use UTC, not your day. Eight places use toISOString(), so between midnight and 5:30 AM in India the "today" date is yesterday. They also ignore the logical day start you set in Settings.Status (Failed, Upcoming), the activity graph and the day popup.
  • MEDIUMMade-up numbers in the header. The header stats show a fixed "42.0H" and fall back to "3" goals. They are placeholders.goalHeaderStats in GoalManagement.
  • MEDIUMSub-goals are not real. A child goal only exists to hold tasks and is never shown as its own card with progress. Task totals only go one level deep.
  • MEDIUMDeleting a goal has no confirmation. It also deletes every sub-goal. Only unlinking a task asks first.
  • MEDIUMDead code. The stored progress value, handleUpdateGoalProgress and the onUpdateProgress prop are never used by the screen. The type category is never used either.
  • LOWConfusing words. "Strategic objective", "operational target", "core target", "strategic timeline". Plain words work better: goal, milestone, task, dates.
  • LOWHeavy before useful. Graphs and analytics come first, and the code is two huge files, so every change is slow and risky.

Rules for the design

  • Status words with fixed rules: Upcoming (starts later), On track (this week's target met or ahead), Behind (below target), Done (all tasks and milestones complete), Missed (past the end date and not done).
  • One color per goal for its ring and bar. The status pill uses the shared green, amber and red.
  • Landscape: goal list on the left, detail on the right.
  • Empty state: three templates and one big "Create your first goal" button, instead of two grey placeholder cards.
  • Graphs and analytics move under a "More" section in the detail page.

Decide before starting

  1. Meaning What is a goal mostly about for you: hours of effort (study 4h a week), tasks done (finish 20 tasks), or milestones (ship v1)? The weekly target and the status rules depend on this. My suggestion is to support hours and milestones, and show tasks done as the progress.
  2. Sub-goals Do you want real sub-goals with their own tasks, or only a simple milestone checklist? The checklist is much simpler.
  3. Graphs Keep the current activity graph and analytics under "More", or remove them to cut about 1,000 lines?
  4. Create flow Three steps, or keep one form but shorter?
  5. Reminders Include weekly and deadline reminders, or skip for now?
  6. Order Start with Phase 0 only (fix the numbers), then look at the result before going on?
